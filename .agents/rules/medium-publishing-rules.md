# Medium Publishing Rules, Link Budget & Delivery Standard

Whenever creating or preparing a Medium article, you MUST strictly adhere to the following rules:

---

## 1. Output Delivery Standard

1. **Title:** Isolated text box.
2. **Subtitle:** Isolated text box.
3. **Tags (5 tags):** Isolated text box.
4. **Image & Alt Text:** Isolated text boxes.
5. **SEO Title & Description:** Isolated text boxes.
6. **Full Article Body:** MUST ALWAYS be provided in clean, standard Markdown inside **one single 4-backtick (` ````markdown `)** copyable block.

---

## 2. Mandatory Link Budget (Strict Enforcement)

Every Medium post MUST contain active, clickable inline links for ALL three tiers:

1. **Tier 1: PowerLab Internal Tools (1–2 links):**
   - Woven contextually into the problem or calculation paragraphs (e.g., `[deterministic generator sizing model](https://www.powelab.org/home-energy/generator-size-calculator)`).

2. **Tier 2: High-Authority External Standards (1–3 MANDATORY ACTIVE HYPERLINKS):**
   - NEVER mention a standard as plain unlinked text. Every cited body MUST have an active external link:
     - NEMA Standards: `[NEMA MG-1 standard](https://www.nema.org/standards/view/motors-and-generators)`
     - IEEE Standards: `[IEEE Std 399 / IEEE Std 485](https://standards.ieee.org)`
     - NFPA / NEC: `[NFPA 70 National Electrical Code](https://www.nfpa.org/codes-and-standards/nfpa-70-standard-development/70)`
     - US Department of Energy: `[U.S. Department of Energy (DOE) EERE](https://www.energy.gov/eere)`
     - NREL / Federal Labs: `[NREL PVWatts](https://pvwatts.nrel.gov)` / `[Idaho National Laboratory](https://avt.inl.gov)`
     - AHRI / ASHRAE: `[AHRI Standard 210/240](https://www.ahrinet.org)` / `[ASHRAE](https://www.ashrae.org)`

3. **Tier 3: Sister Medium Story (0–1 link):**
   - Contextual reference to a previously published Medium article.

---

## 3. Editorial & Anti-Spam Constraints

- **Inline Narrative Integration ONLY:** No standalone "Sources" or "Link Summary" lists at the bottom.
- **Zero Marketing / Promotional Clichés:** No *"100% free"*, *"no signup required"*, *"ultimate guide"*.
- **No Em Dashes (`—`):** Use clean standard punctuation.
