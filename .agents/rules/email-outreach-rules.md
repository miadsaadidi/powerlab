# Email Outreach & Communication Rules (Mandatory & Non-Negotiable)

Before writing, drafting, generating, or sending ANY outreach email, cold pitch, educational message, or editorial communication, you MUST strictly adhere to the following rules and review [`docs/outreach/04_DAILY_5_OUTREACH_TARGETS_AND_PITCHES.md`](file:///d:/powerlab/docs/outreach/04_DAILY_5_OUTREACH_TARGETS_AND_PITCHES.md) and [`docs/14_30_DAY_SEO_AND_BACKLINK_PLAN.md`](file:///d:/powerlab/docs/14_30_DAY_SEO_AND_BACKLINK_PLAN.md).

---

## 1. Core Non-Negotiable Editorial Constraints

1. **Peer-to-Peer Engineering Tone:**
   - Write from the perspective of a computational modeling group offering open datasets, equations, and deterministic models.
   - Maintain the rigorous, collegial tone of researchers or electrical engineers sharing reproducible work.

2. **Intellectual & Scientific Curiosity First:**
   - Every pitch MUST center on a specific, non-trivial engineering challenge, thermodynamic phase boundary, electrochemical loss mechanism, or mathematical divergence between textbook formulas and field measurements (e.g., Peukert derating, cold $V_{oc}$ expansion, inverter tare losses, continuous duty thermal fatigue).

3. **Zero Marketing / Promotional Clichés (Anti-Spam Filter):**
   - NEVER use generic marketing buzzwords or sales language:
     - ❌ *"100% free"*, *"ad-free"*, *"no signup required"* (in marketing pitch style)
     - ❌ *"cutting-edge"*, *"game-changing"*, *"revolutionary"*, *"all-in-one"*
     - ❌ *"check out our site"*, *"please link to us"*

4. **No AI Stylistic Artifacts:**
   - Avoid em dashes (`—`), generic cheerleading, hollow adjectives, conversational fluff, or template filler.
   - Keep links, formulas, and citations clean, concise, and direct.

5. **Strict Follow-Up Discipline:**
   - Exactly **ONE** polite, short follow-up after 7 days (or 72+ business hours).
   - NEVER send a third unprompted message.

## 2. Mandatory 4-Step Curiosity Trigger Structure

Every cold email to an academic, director, or analyst MUST follow this 4-step psychological trigger structure:

1. **The Status Quo / Intuitive Assumption:**
   State what 95% of consumers, textbooks, or commercial calculators assume (e.g. *"Conventional wisdom assumes nominal inverter efficiency is a constant 90%"*).

2. **The Counter-Intuitive Physical Anomaly:**
   Reveal the hidden physical variable that breaks the assumption (e.g. *"Under fractional 50W standby loads, static 20W inverter tare draw causes 25%–40% premature battery depletion"*).

3. **The Material Quantitative Impact:**
   Ground the anomaly with real numbers that matter to an economist, engineer, or policy advocate (e.g. *"Shifts parity by $400/year"* or *"Causes continuous breaker trip under NEC 625.41"*).

4. **The Transparent Deterministic Artifact:**
   Offer PowerLab not as a product, but as an open mathematical reference with transparent, step-by-step derivations and zero black-box calculations.

## 3. Mandatory 2-Step Gate: Discussion & Approval First (NEVER DUMP EMAILS UNPROMPTED)

Whenever the user mentions "outreach", asks for outreach tasks, or explores communication:

1. **Step 1: Present ONLY the high-level Candidate Recipient List:**
   - Output ONLY a concise bulleted list of the candidates:
     - Recipient Name & Title
     - Target Publication / Organization
     - Proposed 1-line Engineering Hook / Topic
   - **DO NOT write, draft, or dump the email bodies.**

2. **Step 2: Pause and Ask for Explicit Approval:**
   - Ask the user to review the recipient list, suggest adjustments, or approve which specific emails to draft.
   - Wait for explicit user confirmation before generating ANY pitch text.
