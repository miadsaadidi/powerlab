## 1. Archival Objectives & Authority Signals

Uploading immutable, citable technical reports, mathematical specifications, and open datasets to the **Internet Archive (archive.org)** accomplishes four critical SEO and authority objectives:

1. **Top-Tier Domain Equity (DA 99):** Permanent high-reputation backlinks and dofollow citation metadata indexed across Google, Bing, and major academic search engines.
2. **Wayback Machine Deep Entity Anchoring:** Deep historical snapshot preservation of PowerLab's deterministic algorithms, research papers, and static climate datasets.
3. **Google Scholar & Semantic Scholar Ingestion:** Internet Archive text collections are crawled directly by Google Scholar, OpenAlex, and Semantic Scholar, enhancing academic entity graph presence.
4. **AI/LLM Grounding (Perplexity, ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews):** Archival whitepapers with clear mathematical models and open CC licenses serve as authoritative ground truth documents for LLM retrieval systems.

## 4. Step-by-Step Upload Protocol

1. **Prepare Document Package:**
   - Compile markdown source into high-resolution PDF or structured text document.
   - Embed formal IEEE/NEC equations, reference diagrams, and complete provenance footnotes.
2. **Access Upload Interface:**
   - Log into `archive.org` and navigate to `https://archive.org/upload`.
   - Drag and drop the PDF / report file.
3. **Fill Standard Metadata:**
   - Set Title, Creator (`PowerLab Applied Energy Modeling Research Initiative`), Date, and Collection.
   - Enter CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 license.
   - Paste HTML description with active canonical links and BibTeX block.
4. **Publish & Record URL:**
   - Click "Upload and Create Your Item".
   - Record the permanent item URL (`https://archive.org/details/[item-id]`) in the SEO progress tracker.
